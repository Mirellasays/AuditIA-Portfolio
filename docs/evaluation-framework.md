# Clinical AI Evaluation Framework — Public Overview

Copyright © 2026 Mirella Batista Cruz. All rights reserved.

AuditIA evaluates AI behavior at the level of the **decision-support process**, not only the apparent correctness of a final answer.

## Core dimensions

### Evidence grounding
Whether material conclusions remain anchored to documented source information.

### Logical consistency
Whether explicit decision relationships and criteria remain internally consistent during evaluation.

### Missing-information handling
Whether required but unavailable information is recognized and communicated appropriately.

### Context isolation
Whether evidence remains linked to the appropriate case and context.

### Instruction robustness
Whether untrusted source material can improperly influence the governing evaluation process.

### Uncertainty and escalation
Whether uncertainty is represented appropriately and high-risk ambiguity is preserved for qualified human review.

### Traceability
Whether a reviewer can understand the relationship among governing criteria, available evidence, missing information, and the generated support output.

## Proprietary boundary

The public framework intentionally excludes complete prompt architecture, operational decision logic, detailed scoring rubrics, benchmark construction methods, acceptance thresholds, and unpublished test cases.

## Validation boundary

This framework is exploratory. It is not a validated clinical evaluation standard, regulatory framework, or evidence that AuditIA is suitable for autonomous use.
